Changelog

What changed

Notable changes per release. Full history lives in the commit log.

3.05 October 2026

New

  • AI providers and model picker. OpenRouter, Gemini, OpenAI, xAI, Anthropic, DeepSeek, Groq, Ollama, a local proxy or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint. Primary and backup order, Fast / Smart / Vision models per provider, keys encrypted with Windows DPAPI.
  • Pro style tab. Real font previews, colors, outlines, shadows and 3D, glow, 10 background shapes, active word, position and tilt. Quick effects and reset per group.
  • Saved styles and last-used style. Save, rename, duplicate, favorite, export and import styles. New videos start from the last style used, remembered separately for vertical and horizontal.
  • 51 templates with a moving gallery. Grouped templates that animate on hover, plus random and mix styles.
  • Copy style from an image. Paste a caption screenshot and get matching colors, a similar font, case, position, highlight and animation.
  • Brand kit with brand colors, fonts, brand words, emoji and tone, plus AI suggestions for words to highlight.
  • Animation studio. 40 ready animations (in, active word, out, loop) with moving previews and a keyframe editor.
  • Sound effects per word. Automatic sounds on key words, numbers, emoji or every page. 12 built-in sounds or your own, on one SFX track.

Fixed

  • Saved styles and the last-used style can no longer be lost when two processes write at the same time.
  • The saved-style card menu stays open while the list refreshes in the background.
  • An empty saved-style list no longer hangs on "loading".

Known limits

  • Heavy templates (3D, long shadows) on very long videos need extra preparation time.
  • Templates designed for 16:9 look small on 9:16. Use the "fits this video" filter.
  • Copy style picks a similar font from the bundled collection, not the exact font.
2.0October 2026

New panel and engine

  • Rebuilt panel with a home screen grouped by task, sibling tabs, a command palette, history, and a shared review list for every tool.
  • Local Python engine with JSON progress, cancel, a persistent worker that keeps Whisper warm, a GPU lock and a disk-space guard.
  • Everything works on a clone. The original sequence and media are never changed.

Tools

  • Silence cutting with an automatic threshold, word guard and four presets.
  • Filler removal with a second listening pass and an acoustic detector.
  • Repeated-take removal that keeps your last take, with a listen-before-apply preview.
  • Profanity censor with beep, mute or duck.
  • Animated word-by-word captions rendered as a transparent overlay.
  • Auto chapters, viral clip finder, auto zoom, auto resize, virtual angles, podcast multicam and b-roll.
  • Clear Voice: DeepFilterNet3 denoise, click and breath removal, loudness per platform and music ducking.
